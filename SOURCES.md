## The card stories

   Republican incumbent Dan Sullivan in Alaska, her party's ninth
   midterm flip, for a 56–44 Senate majority, the largest since 2009.
   *Americas — Reuters, 18 November 03:27 UTC; AP; USA TODAY.*
5. **Voyager 1, one light-day from Earth** — At 5:16 a.m. EST on
   Wednesday, Voyager 1 passed about 16.1 billion miles (25.9bn km)

   signal now takes a full day to reach it. *Space — NASA; Reuters,
   18 November; USA Today; Smithsonian.*

   CDC via Podium News; CIDRAP (WHO remarks, 17 November).*

   *Markets — Reuters, 18 November.*
