1 The Gulf · War — the lead

Iran hits back at US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain

Tehran said it hit Kuwait’s Ali Al Salem base overnight; Bahrain says it has now stopped Iranian attacks three times in a week. Saudi defences downed Houthi missiles aimed at Riyadh — a day after the US struck Qeshm Island.

2 US · Business

Anthropic prices the biggest IPO ever at $83

The $81.3bn offering, enlarged from 825m shares, values Claude’s maker at about $1.5 trillion fully diluted. Shares start trading on Nasdaq on Thursday as ANT.

3 China · Diplomacy

Three leaders, one hug: Trump lands in Shenzhen

Xi greeted Trump on the tarmac with a hug; Putin shook his hand. The three meet Thursday in the first-ever US–China–Russia trilateral.

4 US · Politics

Democrats take the Senate, 56–44

Mary Peltola beat Dan Sullivan in Alaska — the ninth midterm flip — for the party’s biggest majority since 2009.

5 Space · NASA

Voyager 1, one light-day from Earth

At 5:16 a.m. EST it passed 16.1bn miles out; a radio signal now takes a full day to reach it. Launched in 1977.

6 Africa · Health

Ebola passes 11,536 cases in DR Congo

The CDC’s 18 Nov update counts 5,635 deaths; the second-largest outbreak on record. Seven in ten infected under-fives have died.

7 Asia · Markets

Nvidia’s $115.8bn quarter lifts Asia

Revenue doubled year on year; Seoul’s KOSPI rose as much as 3.3% in early trade. Asia-Pacific added 0.9%.

8 UK · Markets

UK prices jump 3.6%; gold slides under $3,800

October inflation hit a one-year high; gold fell to its lowest since September 2025 on a firm dollar.

9 Europe · Brussels

EU proposes a €45bn loan for Ukraine

€25bn for arms, €20bn for the budget — repaid only after Russia pays reparations, the Commission says.

10 Global · Climate

COP31’s draft: 144 paragraphs, no deal yet

Ministers took over the Antalya talks on Tuesday; the first draft, published Monday, splits over fossil fuels and finance. Talks close Friday.

11 Asia · Weather

Cyclone Akvan bears down on India

Now a severe cyclonic storm, 90–100 km/h winds, about 550 km off Andhra Pradesh. Landfall is expected around Friday.

12 Europe · Ukraine war

Ukraine hits two Russian refineries overnight

Ilsky and Afipsky in Krasnodar were struck, Kyiv said, a day after the Kerch Bridge span fell. Berlin talks are set for Thursday.

13 Asia · Finance

DBS opens its vaults to USDC

Singapore’s biggest bank will let institutional clients mint and redeem the stablecoin directly, settling 24/7.

14 Americas · Defence

Hegseth’s Hercules lands in Bogotá

The US defence chief arrived to hand over a first C-130, on loan, with two more expected, Colombia said.

15 US · Economy

Fed minutes: inflation worries linger

October’s minutes, out Wednesday, show some policymakers saw a need for a bigger rate rise.
